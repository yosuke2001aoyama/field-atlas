Why Waymark

ChatGPT answers one question. Waymark remembers the whole journey.

Waymark is for people who travel to understand, not just to visit. It turns real-world noticing into a private map of questions, observations, and reflections.

Coming later: Memory World

Your journey, rebuilt as a memory space.

A future spatial archive of places, questions, and reflections: first a Memory Board, later a 3D Memory Walk. It will help you revisit the real world, not escape it.
