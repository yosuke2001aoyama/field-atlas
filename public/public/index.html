What Waymark U.S. Does

Capture

Save place-based notes, community logs, transcripts, uploads, coordinates, moods, tags, and themes.

Organize

Use local SQLite storage, maps, filters, mock AI summaries, and before-arrival briefs.

Publish Carefully

Create public versions that generalize location, date, identity, affiliation, and sensitive detail.

Privacy First

Waymark U.S. preserves the distinction between private raw notes, AI-organized personal knowledge, and anonymized public storytelling. Exact locations, raw transcripts, private names, affiliations, and real-time itinerary details should stay private by default.
